# Open Issues Register

**Status:** maintained alongside the design, which is at revision 29; its entries were recorded against revision 9 and the implementation-plan review. The prototype (`gatedtrain` 0.7.2) implements revision 29 except the training-seed unit of revision 20 (05 §3.3). Each entry is a review finding deliberately deferred to a later phase. An entry closes when the phase that owns it updates the relevant sub-design; until then the sub-design text stands as written.

**Gate** is the phase whose entry (or a named event) must not pass while the issue is open. **Owner** is a maintainer role, not a person; maintainers take on the role when the phase's scope is decided. Site roles (site administrators, health-service operators) are prerequisites that an operator's site provides.

## Calibration items (proposed defaults to confirm in shadow mode)

`fenced_compute_max_s`, `fenced_hold_max_s` (30 min), lease TTL and renewal period, bulk release rate (20 per cluster per 10 min), hardware-evidenced resubmit budget (6 + nodes/64 per 24 h), correlated-stall threshold k = 3, `diag_verdict_wait_s` (5 min), adapter canary period (48 h), and the G0 GPU-hour cap (2,000). Owner: operations maintainer (control-plane values) and G0 maintainer (cap).
